# Case Study

## Documentation Challenge

A permissions-based interface can be difficult to document because
different users may see different options.

## Approach

I structured the content around administrator tasks instead of
describing every screen.

The guide separates account creation, role modification, account
deactivation, and troubleshooting into independent tasks.

## Skills Demonstrated

- Task-oriented writing
- Role-based documentation
- Information organization
- Notes and warnings
- Troubleshooting
